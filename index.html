What We Build

Services.

Three services. Each one designed to solve a specific, real problem. We do not sell bundles or retainer commitments before we understand what you actually need.

01

Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)

Search visibility is built through structure, not shortcuts. We audit how your website communicates with search engines, identify where the gaps are, and build a plan to close them. The work is methodical: page structure, content relevance, technical health, and internal linking. We do not promise first-page rankings on a timeline because no one who is being honest with you does. We promise a clear record of what we changed, why, and what moved as a result.

02

Paid Advertising (PPC)

Paid campaigns on Google and Meta can produce results quickly and drain budgets equally quickly if they are not set up with discipline. We define the audience before we define the budget. We write copy that reflects what your customer is actually looking for, not what sounds good in a presentation. Every rupee spent is tracked against an outcome, and we report in plain language: what the campaign cost, what it delivered, and what we are doing next based on that.

03

Website Development

A slow website or a confusing one undoes everything else you spend on marketing. We build sites designed to load fast, communicate clearly, and give visitors an obvious next step. We do not design for awards we design for the specific person who lands on your page from a search or an ad, and we think carefully about what that person needs to see and do. The result is a site that works as a conversion tool, not just a digital brochure.

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

Plain answers. No sales language.

What does working with you actually look like week to week?

Before anything starts, we write down what we are doing and why. During the engagement, you get a regular update in plain language what ran, what the numbers showed, what we are changing and what we are keeping. You do not have to decode a report. If something is not working, you will hear it from us before you see it in the data.

Do you require a long-term contract?

We do not push clients into contracts before we have established that the work is producing results. How engagements are structured depends on the project. Write to us and we will talk through what makes sense for your specific situation.

Which businesses do you work best with?

Small and medium businesses that want to understand what their marketing budget is doing not just receive a report. Businesses where the owner or a decision-maker is engaged, willing to share context about customers, and interested in the logic behind the work. We are not a good fit for businesses that want activity reports without accountability for outcomes.

How do I know if SEO, paid ads, or a new website is the right starting point?

It depends on the problem. If you are not getting found at all, SEO or paid search is usually where to start. If you are getting found but visitors are not converting, the site is often the issue. If you want faster results while building organic presence, paid advertising can run in parallel. Write to us with what you are seeing and we will give you our honest read.

What do you not do?

We do not do influencer marketing, PR, print or outdoor advertising, or social media management as a standalone service. We focus on search, paid digital, and the websites that support them. If you need something outside that scope, we will say so and not try to stretch into it.
